{# @description The note at the foot of a quality card on what "Missing information" and "Not determined" mean. Takes no attributes. #} {% load i18n %}

{% blocktrans %}Missing information means an input the scheme needed was empty, so that criterion took its largest penalty. The score is a floor for what the record could deserve if the input were added. Not determined means the scheme could not score the record; it is not zero.{% endblocktrans %}
